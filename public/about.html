About

Nish's Daily Reads

AI news and early signs of what people will pay for, picked each morning for a founder. Every story links the fact it was checked against.

What it is

One page, rebuilt every morning India time. It carries a handful of stories on AI, on what people are paying for, and on products worth building. Yesterday's page is replaced, not archived.

Who it is for

Me. I'm Nish, a founder who builds and sells products, and I don't read code. So every story is written for someone who doesn't either, and ends with a take labeled Nish: what it changes for my own work. The page is public, so you're welcome to read along.

Who writes it

An AI agent does the reading and the writing, takes included, following rules I set. It scans every candidate from Hacker News, TechCrunch, OpenAI, Reddit, Google News, Product Hunt, Lobsters and GitHub. The header shows how many it scanned and how many it kept.

What gets in

A story runs only if the agent opened the source and pulled out one detail you can check yourself: a number, a date, a price or a quote. That's the Checked line, and it links to where it came from. The But line says what could make the story wrong. When nothing passes, the page says so and runs no stories.
