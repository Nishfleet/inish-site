# Lane 1 report: complete the share-metadata head contract (og:site_name, og:locale, twitter:image:alt) — 2026-08-21

## Item

Self-directed cycle: walk the live product for public-promise gaps and UX
breakage (tiers 1-2), then tiers 4-7. No free backlog item — the walk itself
finds the work.

## Verdict

Shipped. The live walk found every tier 1-2 surface healthy; the gap was in
tier 7 share metadata. The generated head already declared
`og:title`/`og:description`/`og:image` (+alt/type/width/height) and Twitter's
`card`/`title`/`description`/`image`, but it did not declare `og:site_name`
(unfurlers on Facebook/LinkedIn could not name the source), `og:locale` (the
page language was unstated), or `twitter:image:alt` (X's card reader received
no alt text even though `og:image:alt` was present). None of the open PRs
#100-#108 owns these tags.

## Changes

- `scripts/build_daily.py` — `page()` now emits `og:site_name`
  ("Nish's Daily Reads"), `og:locale` (`en_US`), and
  `twitter:image:alt`. The previous hardcoded `og:image:alt` literal became a
  shared `image_alt` constant so the two alt tags can never drift apart.
- `index.html` — regenerated from the builder (committed surface stays
  byte-exact to the newest accepted edition; `feed.xml`, `latest.json`, and
  `sitemap.xml` regenerated byte-identical).
- `tests/test_build_daily.py` — new
  `test_head_declares_site_name_locale_and_twitter_image_alt` pins all three
  tags and asserts the og/twitter alt values are identical.
