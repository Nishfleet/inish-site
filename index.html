OpenAI finally ships ChatGPT and Codex for Linux

OpenAI has released a worldwide preview of its desktop app for Linux. The app includes ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex, giving Linux users a native desktop route instead of relying on the browser.

Checked The preview supports Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04 LTS, Debian 13, and Fedora 43 and 44.

Nish I read this as ecosystem catch-up rather than a novelty launch. A native Linux app removes one more reason for technical teams to standardize on a competitor's desktop experience.

But It is still a preview and the supported distribution list is narrower than the full Linux universe.

River AI raises $1.1B to make models personally trainable

River AI, founded by former xAI researcher Igor Babuschkin, has raised a huge round only two months after coming out of stealth. Its pitch is that people and companies should train open models into assistants they can actually own and improve.

Checked River says its API supports reinforcement learning and LoRA fine-tuning, and that a complex training run can take 15 to 20 minutes without an infrastructure team.

Nish I do not see River's funding headline as the main signal. I see a shift from asking people to write better prompts toward giving them a model they can teach. That is a much larger product surface for Tiny Studio to watch.

But The speed and cost claims are company claims, and the product is too young to show whether the promised training workflow works outside demos.
