Context

The pattern in the work matters more than the category.

I keep drifting toward products with clearer stakes. Some of them become calmer consumer apps. Some of them become more operational software. The job is still the same: make the product easier to trust.

  • I like products with a real job, not vague ambition.
  • I care more about proof and clarity than feature theater.
  • Applied AI should make the work self-serve and agent-assisted, not just add a chat box.
  • I want the work to feel current without turning loud or fake.
  • Tiny Studio is the company layer. This site is where the builder voice sits.

Agent brief

A cleaner handoff for useful AI work.

Every serious project should become easier to use without a meeting: clear context in, bounded agent work out, and a human gate where the stakes are money, privacy, security, legal exposure, or public claims.

Self-serve first

Onboarding, support, fulfillment, reporting, and proof should be available as product surfaces, not only as founder-led explanation.

Agentic where it matters

Agents should gather context, draft or execute bounded workflow steps, and route work between cheaper models and frontier models based on risk and required intelligence.

Human gates stay explicit

The system can move faster only when approval points are clear: payments, private data, security, legal exposure, public copy, and deploys stay owner-reviewed.
